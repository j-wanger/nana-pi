## The record (from the draft, cut to its read fields)

- **Where:** `<git common dir of --main>/nana/land-records.jsonl`, found once through `repoKeyOf(main)` with `git:` stripped, as build-report does. Why: every worktree shares it, cleanup keeps it, it sits beside the build ledger so build-report reads both with one lookup, a suite's fixture lands write into the fixture's own `.git`, and `repoKeyOf` already handles a second spelling (R-1355). Not `~/.pi/agent` (mixes repositories) and not the suite-runs folder (the 300 cap is the audit's complaint).
- **Two kinds, build-records form (D2/D3 precedent).** A `start` line once main's common folder is found, an `end` line when the attempt ends. A start with no end reads `no end`: interrupted, killed hard, crashed or still running. nana-land runs the suite through `spawnSync`, so a Ctrl-C kills node before any handler runs (inferred); the start line is the only honest way to see an interrupted land. No signal handler is added.
- **Start fields:** `v` 1, `kind`, `attempt` (randomUUID), `startedAt`, `lane` (the source tree's branch `feat/<lane>` under the lane-name pattern, else null), `tip` (source HEAD full id, or null).
- **End fields:** `v`, `kind`, `attempt`, `endedAt`, `durationMs`, `outcome` (`landed` | `refused`), `stage` (null, or `preflight` | `ci` | `suite` | `merge`), `reason` (null, or the refusal's first line without `refused: `, capped), `ci` (`passed` | `skipped` | `refused` | `not reached`), `ciWaitMs`, `suiteMs`. Stage boundaries: everything before the CI scope read is `preflight`; the scope read, gh and `judgeRuns` are `ci`; the suite result is `suite`; everything after the suite passed (second tally read, rechecks, merge, containment) is `merge`.
- **Every attempt:** landed (end after the CI-records step); refused before the suite (`suiteMs: null`, `ci: "not reached"` or `"refused"`); refused by CI (`stage: "ci"`, `suiteMs: null`); refused by the suite (`suiteMs` set, reason `suite failed (exit N)` only, never the suite output); refused after the suite (`stage: "merge"`); interrupted (start only). Argument refusals in `parseLandArgs` and a `--main` outside any repository write nothing and print nothing extra (no clone to write to). `nana-land cleanup` writes nothing.
- **CI wait:** `max(updated_at − created_at)` over the judged runs, in ms; null when CI did not pass or when any judged run's creation or last-update time is missing, unreadable or gives a negative span. `judgeRuns`' ok result gains the judged runs so the wait can be computed. The CI verdict and its lines never depend on it.
- **Clock:** one injected `now` (default `() => new Date()`) for `startedAt`, `endedAt`, `durationMs` and `suiteMs`, as `keepLandCiRecords` injects its clock.
- **Atomicity and cap:** one `appendFileSync` per line, no lock (build-records D2); a torn line is skipped and counted. Both appends sit outside the review-ledger lock, so the ledger never lengthens the lock hold. No retention, so land records outlive the 300-run cap; reads are bounded at `LAND_LEDGER_READ_MAX_BYTES`, chosen 32 MiB: under 1 KB an attempt after the cuts and about 20 attempts a day (31 suite-reaching lands in 2.3 days measured, refusals inferred) gives over four years.
- **The record line never contains** `refused:`, `Landed:` or `push command:`; existing tests assert those absent from some outputs.

## Threat model

In scope, honest variation in our tools' output: two lands of one clone at once; a land killed mid-suite (Ctrl-C, closed terminal, SIGKILL); a suite killed by signal; `.git/nana` as a file or the ledger as a folder; torn, other-schema or orphan lines; a source tree detached or off `feat/`; a `--main` outside any repository; `--tree` and `--main` in different clones (R-1009: the record goes to main's clone); a run object without times, or with a negative span; a re-run attempt; a ledger over its cap; Windows paths (8.3, case) and a second spelling of main (R-1355). Out: deliberate forgery, such as a hand-edited ledger or forged CI times. A finding outside this model is a residual.

## Known limits (the seat writes them before the final round)

- Argument refusals and a `--main` outside any repository are not recorded.
- `no end` cannot tell an interrupted land from a running one.
- A land stopped during its CI-records read has merged but reads `no end`.
- The CI wait is recorded only when CI passed, and a re-run inflates it with the gap before the re-run was asked for.
- A merge-stage refusal may follow a completed fast-forward.
- Records are per clone and start at this lane.
- Past the read cap, every land's last line says `lane line unavailable`; there is no retention.
- The page shows `age not measured: clone store` for the land records.
- No lock, so a same-instant tear is possible (inferred never on a local disk).
