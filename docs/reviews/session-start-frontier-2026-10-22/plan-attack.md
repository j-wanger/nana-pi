That puts the tree check near 530 ms there (inferred). A slow start under the six parallel SessionStart hooks then turns the whole frontier block into the unavailable marker, not only the count.
  - Change. Remove D8, `DELIVERABLES_TIMEOUT_MS`, `DELIVERABLES_LINE`, `LEDGER_FILE`, pairs 9 and 10, the process-edge known limit, the `process` effect on lib/frontier-summary.mjs, and the not-counted lines. ## S (should)

S1. The shared 10,000-character hook cap can push the objective out. The frontier goes into the objective hook's own stdout. Claude Code caps one hook's plain stdout at 10,000 chars. Over the cap, the whole stdout becomes a file path and a 2,000-character preview (recorded in docs/reviews/install-check-2026-10-12/CHANGE.md:255; the install check fails an entry over it, packages/nana-setup/lib/install-check.mjs:469). The objective block alone, built from a temp home with lines of N chars: N=500 gives 3,480; N=1,000 gives 5,480; N=1,500 (objective.ts LINE_CAP) gives 7,434. The local maximum today is 2,478 (14 projects). So realistic objectives (lines under 500, objective.ts:66) stay under the cap. But an objective from 6,000 to 10,000 chars, delivered whole today, would lose its governing text to a preview once 4,000 frontier chars join it. The known limit ("An objective near its own 12,000 cap already passes it alone") skips that band.
  Change. Budget the frontier against what the objective printed. Give `runHookProducer` an `args` option and have it return the length it wrote. The hook then passes `--max <9,000 minus that length>` to the frontier producer, which caps its block to it, or prints one omitted line. The 9,000 margin is SHARED_MEMORY_OUTPUT_BUDGET's (shared-memory-config.mjs:9-11; R-2346). Add a case to R-2613's file: an objective stub printing 8,000 chars plus a live frontier gives stdout of 10,000 chars or fewer with the objective whole. Add the pair "the hook writes both blocks with no shared budget".
